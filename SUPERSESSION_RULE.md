# Ultimate Loop — Supersession Rule

Status: `CANONICAL`

## Canonical termination rule

**Ultimate Loop ends its claim to canonical supremacy the moment a demonstrably superior development protocol is proven.**

Ultimate Loop has no permanent right to exist.

`ULTIMATE LOOP != IMMORTAL`

`SUPERIOR PROTOCOL PROVEN → ULTIMATE LOOP YIELDS`

## What counts as proven

A successor is not superior merely because it is newer, more popular, uses a stronger model, has better branding, wins one benchmark, or declares itself superior.

A challenger must preserve or improve the protected human-important outcome across the material obligations inherited from Ultimate Loop, including where applicable:

- known failure and autopsy memory;
- evidence and reproducibility requirements;
- authority boundaries;
- recovery and reconstruction capability;
- provider and implementation replaceability;
- real-world destructive cases and material workloads;
- whole-life cost;
- operator burden;
- safety and resilience.

A successor must demonstrate at least one **material improvement** in the declared mission or materially broader capability. Merely matching Ultimate Loop does not establish supersession.

A challenger may use a radically different structure. It does not need to copy Ultimate Loop's names, loops, agents, tools, or implementation.

`DIFFERENT != INFERIOR`

`NEW != SUPERIOR`

`EQUAL != SUPERIOR`

`CLAIMED SUPERIORITY != PROVEN SUPERIORITY`

`GOAL SHIFT != METHOD SUPERIORITY`

## Anti-self-preservation rule

Ultimate Loop may not exempt itself from comparison.

It may not require a challenger to use Ultimate Loop merely to be considered valid.

The comparison target is the protected outcome and inherited material obligations, not loyalty to the incumbent method.

## Handover

Proof of superiority and operational handover are separate.

`SUPERIOR PROTOCOL PROVEN → SUPERSEDED_PENDING_HANDOVER`

`AUTHORITY + CONTINUITY PASS → RETIRED / SUCCESSOR_CANONICAL`

The old method may remain a bounded caretaker only long enough to avoid a responsibility gap. It no longer has permanent entitlement to remain canonical.

A successor must inherit or intentionally disposition the protected outcome, canonical evidence, known failure memory, authority boundaries, and reconstruction path before handover is considered complete.

## Lineage after supersession

Supersession does not erase history.

Useful evidence, failures, reconstruction material, and lineage remain available to successors and future challengers.

`METHOD MAY YIELD`

`LINEAGE MUST SURVIVE`

`PROTECTED OUTCOME OUTRANKS METHOD IDENTITY`
